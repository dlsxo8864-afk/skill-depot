Vertical 9:16 Korean live-action psychological drama, single continuous generation, nine shots, hard cuts only. ARRI ALEXA, 35mm spherical primes, Kodak Portra 400 tone curve, fine 35mm grain, natural skin texture with visible pores. Realistic office lighting: low late-afternoon window light mixed with flat cool overhead fluorescent. Muted desaturated palette of slate grey, ivory and warm neutral skin. Restrained performance throughout: every face in this piece is calm on the surface, the mouth closed at rest, the eyes bright and dry with the lower lids still and the cheeks low and unmoving. Subjects are held in the upper two-thirds of the frame with clean empty negative space along the bottom edge.

REFERENCE ROLES
@Image1 is the empty office corridor: it defines the architecture, the grey fabric partition walls, the glass meeting rooms, the floor and the daylight of every present-day shot. @Image2 is the small glass-walled meeting room: it defines the room, the table and the cold flat daylight of the flashback only. @Image3 is used only as a crop source for Subject1's face, hairline and clothing colours — head and shoulders only. @Image4 is used only as a crop source for Subject2's face, hairline and clothing colours — head and shoulders only. Every shot's background is the location named in that shot, never a plain grey field.

Define the man in @Image3 as Subject1: Korean man, 37 years old, angular bony face, high cheekbones, short neat black hair with a clean side part, medium lean build, navy blue cotton button-up shirt with sleeves rolled to the forearm, charcoal grey slacks, black leather strap wristwatch on his left wrist. Subject1 is never clean-shaven-baby-faced, never has long hair, never has a beard or moustache, and never wears a tie, a jacket, a light-coloured shirt or a jumper over the navy shirt.
Define the woman in @Image4 as Subject2: Korean woman, 28 years old, oval face with soft cheeks, black blunt chin-length bob touching her shoulders with a soft side fringe, natural minimal makeup, ivory silk-blend blouse with a soft open collar, dark grey tailored slacks. Subject2 never has hair below the jaw, never has a ponytail, a bun or an updo, and never wears a dark top or a jacket.
Faces, hairstyles, builds and costumes are identical in every shot; nothing is ever exchanged between the two of them.
Subject2's small brown cardboard box with rope handles belongs to her alone and is carried in both of her hands at waist height. There is exactly one box in the entire piece; it never leaves her hands and is never set down; Subject1 never holds, touches or takes it.

SCENE AXIS — holds for every corridor shot. Subject1 stands on the frame-right side of the corridor axis and Subject2 on the frame-left side, and the camera never crosses the line between them. The low late-afternoon window light sits beyond the frame edge at frame right throughout the corridor.

Exactly three people appear anywhere in this piece: Subject1; Subject2; and, in Shots 2 and 3 only, one person seated at the meeting table, seen only from behind — the back of her head, one shoulder and one forearm, a woman with dark hair tied low at the nape and a plain mid-grey knit top, no badge and no lanyard, never her face, never standing, never in any other shot. Nobody else: no colleagues, no passers-by, no reflections of other people, no figures in the corridor behind them.

Shot 1 [0-4s] | close-up, eye level — HELD
Subject1's face fills the frame from the shoulders up and his lips part a few millimetres as though a sentence has started, then press shut again and stay shut; one dry swallow moves his throat. His eyes stay fixed on the person off frame, the outer corners tightening, and in the last second his pupils shift once, very slightly. The camera makes one continuous very slow push-in of about five percent of the frame height across the whole four seconds, and that push is its only movement — no pan, no zoom, no handheld sway, no reframing. At the lower-left edge of the frame, soft and completely out of focus, sit the curve of an ivory-clad shoulder and the corner of a brown cardboard box. Low warm light from frame right edges one cheekbone and leaves the other side in cool shade. The corridor behind him is empty and still.

Shot 2 [4-6s] | extreme close-up at tabletop height — BRIEF
A single-frame hard cut into the meeting room of @Image2, graded distinctly colder and desaturated with a faint green fluorescent cast. The frame holds the pale grey laminate tabletop and one hand: Subject1's hand, his navy cuff and black leather watch at the wrist, slides a closed folder across the table away from him, then lifts off it and stops still in the air above the surface. The folder is a plain navy board cover with one pale grey band running along its top edge and a single blank cream label patch at its lower left; every surface of it is flat unbroken colour with nothing printed, written or stamped on any part of it. Out of focus in the background, his own face and navy shoulder are faintly visible above the table edge, and he is looking down at the folder.

Shot 3 [6-9s] | medium close-up, three-quarter front — STANDARD
Same room, same cold flat light. The back of the seated colleague's head, her shoulder and one forearm sit soft and out of focus in the near foreground, opposite Subject1; she sits still and does not turn, and her face is out of frame for the whole shot. Across the table Subject1 lifts his head from the folder and looks at her. His face stays flat and closed: the mouth level and shut, the eyes steady on her, one slow blink. He does not soften and he does not look back down at the folder. Low room tone only; the room is otherwise still.

Shot 4 [9-13s] | medium two-shot at chest height, camera perpendicular to the line between them — STANDARD
A single-frame hard cut back to the corridor of @Image1, in warmer late-afternoon light: her last working day, a few days after the meeting room. Subject2 stands at frame left holding the brown cardboard box at her waist with both hands, her body angled toward frame right. Subject1 stands at frame right in three-quarter back view, about one and a half metres from her across a band of empty pale grey floor, already beginning to walk away. Low warm light comes through the glass rooms at frame right and lays a long soft sheen on the empty floor between them; the far end of the corridor stays brighter than the foreground. She lifts her chin toward him and her mouth begins to form a word and stops before any sound; at that same instant he stops half-turned, one shoulder toward her. Her fingers close harder around the rope handle of the box until the knuckles go pale, and then he comes slowly around to face her. Nobody else is in the corridor.

Shot 5 [13-18s] | over-the-shoulder close-up — LONG
A single-frame hard cut. The camera sits just behind and beside Subject2's left shoulder; her ivory sleeve and the corner of the brown box fill the soft left edge of the frame and stay utterly still. Subject1's face is at close range. He comes around to her in one quarter turn and stops. His gaze drops off her eyes and goes past her throat, and he does not bring it back. His right hand hangs at his side; the index and middle fingers curl inward and grip the outer seam of his slacks for one beat, then let go. The same low warm light from frame right edges his cheek and jaw and leaves the near side of his face in cool shade. The corridor behind him is empty and still.

Shot 6 [18-21s] | medium close-up, eye level — HELD
A single-frame hard cut. Subject2's face at close range, the low warm light from frame right edging the back of her hair. She looks screen right, off frame toward him. Only the outer corners of her mouth lift a few millimetres and stay there; the lips remain pressed closed, the teeth never show, the cheeks barely rise and the skin around her eyes stays smooth. Her brow stays smooth and her eyes stay wide and calm and dry. She blinks once and keeps waiting. The corridor behind her is empty and still.

Shot 7 [21-23s] | three-quarter close-up, camera set slightly to his right — STANDARD
A single-frame hard cut. Subject1's face, the same low warm light from frame right cross-lighting him from beyond the frame edge behind him. He looks screen left, off frame toward her. His gaze drops away for one beat and returns to her. His mouth opens a few millimetres as though to answer, and closes again without a sound. The corridor behind him is empty and still.

Shot 8 [23-28s] | two-shot — LONG
The camera begins just behind and beside Subject1's right shoulder: his navy shoulder soft at the left edge of frame, Subject2 facing camera across the gap. Over the whole five seconds it dollies back on the perpendicular — one continuous slow and steady retreat of about three metres — until both figures stand small and upright, each about one quarter of the frame height, in a wide vertical frame: the grey partition walls and glass meeting rooms running away on either side, warm late-afternoon light glowing at the far end of the corridor. No zoom, no pan, no reframing and no cut inside this shot; the figures shrink only because the camera travels. She holds the brown box at her waist and adjusts her fingertips once on the rope handle, and blinks. He shifts his weight by a centimetre and does not step forward. Neither of them closes the distance and neither turns away. Nobody else is in the corridor and the floor between them stays empty.

Shot 9 [28-30s] | extreme close-up, eye level — BRIEF
A single-frame hard cut. Subject1's eyes fill the frame, framed so the brow line and the bridge of his nose stay just inside the edges; the whites are clear and dry, the lashes still. His pupils move once toward frame left in the smallest search, and then hold absolutely still on the person off frame. The picture stays on his eyes through the final frame of the clip: no fade, no dim, no cut to black, no title card.

Every transition in this piece is a single-frame hard cut. No dissolve and no fade anywhere. No subtitles, no captions, no on-screen text, no lettering, no signage, no name badges, no logos and no watermark anywhere in frame. Every wall, door, glass panel, partition, chair and furniture surface is plain unbroken material — matte white plaster, grey fabric, clear glass, dark grey plastic — and the only marks on any surface are pale reflections of the window light and the soft falloff of shadows. Their hands stay where they are written: hers on the rope handles of the box at her waist, his hanging at his side or gripping his own trouser seam; there is no physical contact between them at any moment — no touch, no embrace, no holding hands, no reaching hand. Both of them stay facing each other and stay in frame until the picture ends. Face stable and undeformed, natural body proportions, natural hands with five fingers, consistent costume and hairstyle throughout, no morphing, no warping, no flicker, no stretching, no duplicated limbs. Vertical 9:16 framing held from the first frame to the last. Sound: nothing at all — no music, no dialogue, no voices, no speech, no narration in any language, no sound effects, no footsteps, no room tone. Nobody in this piece speaks and no lip-sync occurs; every movement of a mouth is silent breath.
